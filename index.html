Our Story

Built for the workshop, not the warehouse.

Swadeshi Bazaar exists because the hardest part of an artisan's day was never the craft itself — it was everything after. The photo, the listing, the price, the sale. We handle that part, in the artisan's own language, so the only thing left to focus on is the work their hands already know how to do.
