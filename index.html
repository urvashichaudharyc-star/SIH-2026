The craft is ready. The market isn't.

Four everyday obstacles keep an artisan's products off the shelves they deserve to be on.

Manual cataloging is hard

Photographing, describing, and tagging dozens of products by hand eats hours an artisan doesn't have to spare.

No direct market access

Without a storefront of their own, most sales pass through middlemen who keep the larger share of the margin.

Language and digital literacy barriers

Typing out a product listing in English shuts out artisans who think and speak in their own language.

Poor product presentation

A blurry photo with no story behind it can't compete with a polished listing from a bigger seller.

What the app does

Four features carry the entire path from raw product to finished listing.

Snap & Catalog

AI vision does the paperwork

Photograph a product and the app generates its title, description, category, and tags on its own.

Voice-first

Built for the vernacular

Adding a product, checking an order, replying to a buyer — every action can be spoken and heard in the artisan's own language.

Direct linkage

Straight to the buyer

Listings connect directly to buyers, cutting out the middlemen standing between the workshop and the sale.

Auto-storytelling

Every craft gets its backstory

The app writes the material, technique, and regional origin into each listing, so it reads like it belongs in a gallery.
